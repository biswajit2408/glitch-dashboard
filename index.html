Setup
Connect Google Sheets — one time only
What you'll set up
A single Google Spreadsheet acts as your entire backend — 4 tabs: Content Plans, Ideas Library, Analytics Log, Settings. Free forever. No server needed. You own all your data.
📋 Step by step — follow exactly
1
Go to sheets.google.com → click Blank spreadsheet → name it "Glitch and Games — Creator Dashboard"
2
In your new sheet → click Extensions (top menu) → Apps Script → a new tab opens with a code editor
3
Delete everything in the editor → paste the entire contents of google-apps-script.js (from your project ZIP) → press Ctrl+S to save
4
Click Deploy (top right) → New deployment → Type: Web app → Execute as: Me → Who has access: Anyone → click Deploy
5
Google will ask you to authorise → click Authorize access → choose your Gmail → click Advanced → Go to project (unsafe) → Allow
6
Copy the Web app URL shown after deployment → it looks like:
https://script.google.com/macros/s/YOUR_ID_HERE/exec
7
Paste that URL below and click Connect & Setup
🔌 Connect your Google Sheets
